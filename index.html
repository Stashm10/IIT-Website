IIT

Spectrum Observatory — 44–900 MHz

Illinois Institute of Technology  ·  Chicago, IL  ·  299 observed days in 2018

Real radio frequency spectrum measurements collected by the IIT Spectrum Observatory, located on the rooftop of IIT Tower at 10 W. 35th Street, Chicago. The spectrum is split into the analysis groups used in Wei & Hood (IEEE RTC 2026). Click any band to see its power distribution for every observed day of 2018, pooled by month or for the whole year.

Draft band plan. DRAFT - group list taken from the Wei & Hood IEEE RTC 2026 poster; the breakdown inside each group is a proposal and must be confirmed with the professor / PhD student before publication.
